# Minimum V4 shooting rules specification for calculator V1

## Scope and evidence

This specification defines Flames of War Fourth Edition (V4) shooting rules relevant to the current direct-fire calculator and the requested infantry artillery calculator. The direct-fire product accepts resolved facts from the user; it does not identify weapons or Units. Artillery facts below cover only bombardments against Infantry Teams. It does not specify aircraft shooting, assaults, or vehicle-target resolution.

## Artillery bombardment against Infantry Teams (V1)

The official QRS is a summary, not the complete rulebook. The following is limited to what its V4 artillery headings establish. Do not infer a full game simulation from the bombardment probability calculator; targeting legality, spotting, and many army-book special rules require more inputs than the requested V1 interface.

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible bombardment | `VERIFIED_V4` | Any Team with an Artillery weapon (ROF marked “artillery” or “salvo”) may fire a Bombardment instead of Shooting; a Unit with Artillery weapons is an Artillery Unit (V4 rulebook p.66, “Who Can Bombard,” official extract source 7). Other Teams can still Shoot normally. |
| Whether Unit artillery weapons must fire together | `UNRESOLVED` | V4 QRS/extract available for this audit verifies who can bombard but does not reproduce the full sentence on whether every Artillery Team in the Unit must fire as part of that Bombardment or not fire. The full rulebook text is needed before enforcing all-or-none Unit participation. Until then the gun count may be entered as the number actually participating, without claiming Unit eligibility validation. |
| Aiming point and template | `VERIFIED_V4` | The Spotting Team selects an Aiming Point in its Line of Sight; an Artillery Template is 6in/15cm square and a Salvo Template is 10in/25cm square. Template danger-close restrictions: 4in/10cm for Artillery, 6in/15cm for Salvo, 8in/20cm for aircraft (V4 rulebook pp.66, 100, official extract source 7; QRS “Select Aiming Point,” p.67). V1 accepts the already-established number of Infantry Teams under the template; it does not calculate table geometry or validate the aiming point. |
| Range In and attempts | `VERIFIED_V4` | Make up to three Range In attempts, rolling against the Artillery Unit's Skill; +1 to the score required if the template would cover Short or Tall Terrain or Smoke, and +1 at Night (QRS p.68). Success on attempt one has no hit penalty; attempt two adds +1 to score needed To Hit; attempt three adds +2 (QRS p.69). |
| Teams under template | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll separately to hit each Team under the template (QRS “Roll to Hit,” p.69; Early/Late QRS p.48: “all Teams under Template”). Whether a base only partially covered by the template counts as under it is not explicit in the cited official summaries and remains `UNRESOLVED`. For probability arithmetic, supplied team count means teams adjudicated by the player as under the template. |
| Artillery To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | Base is the target Team’s Is Hit On number (Veteran 4+, Trained 3+, Conscript 2+); add +1 for ranging in on attempt two or a Repeat Bombardment whose Spotting Team cannot see the Aiming Point; add +2 for attempt three (QRS p.69). The Artillery To Hit target is not the Direct Fire To Hit target and does not use its normal long-range/concealment modifiers. |
| Number of weapons firing | `VERIFIED_V4` | With 1 or 2 weapons, re-roll successful To Hit rolls; with 5 or more, re-roll failed To Hit rolls (QRS p.69, “Number of Weapons Firing”). The sheet states no modifier for 3–4 weapons. Re-rolls affect each eligible hit die; they are not extra shots. |
| Artillery Firepower and range | `VERIFIED_V4` | Use the weapon's artillery/bombardment Firepower profile with the V4 artillery adjustment table: printed FP 1+→1+, 2+→2+, 3+→3+, 4+→3+, 5+→4+, 6→4+ (Early/Late QRS p.48, “Arsenal Firepower / Adjusted Firepower”). This changes the Firepower value; no range modifier to artillery Firepower or Infantry Other Saves is stated. The separate artillery Anti-tank adjustment is irrelevant to Infantry-only V1. Range eligibility still applies when selecting a legal Aiming Point. Rocket Launchers retain their unadjusted Firepower (V4 EW/LW FAQ, “Artillery,” Rocket Launchers); omit rocket launchers or add that specific handling. |
| Infantry Other Save and casualty sequence | `VERIFIED_V4` | For Infantry, the ordinary Other Save is 3+ (Early/Late QRS p.39); 4+ and 5+ in that table apply to other target classes, not Infantry. Roll the save: success means unharmed; failure means destroyed unless the Team has Bulletproof Cover, in which case the attacker must pass Firepower to destroy it (same QRS; V4 FAQ “Bulletproof Cover”). Artillery’s save exception states no Bulletproof Cover behind terrain (artillery QRS p.70), so ordinary terrain cover does not grant the Firepower gate against bombardment. |
| Dug In versus not Dug In | `VERIFIED_V4` | Successful Dig In places the platoon in Foxholes and grants Bulletproof Cover (Open Fire V4 booklet p.14, source 10; full rulebook Dig In/terrain rules govern actual eligibility). Dug In itself does not change artillery To Hit: bombardment To Hit uses target Team Is Hit On plus only artillery modifiers listed at rulebook p.69/QRS. Foxhole Bulletproof Cover is not “behind terrain” cover, so the QRS exception does not remove it: after failing the 3+ save, the attacker must pass the bombardment weapon's Firepower test to destroy; failing that test, the Team survives. If not Dug In and not otherwise in Bulletproof Cover, a failed 3+ save destroys without Firepower. If protection comes from being behind terrain, that protection does not apply to artillery. |
| Dug In input sufficiency | `UNRESOLVED` | Binary “Dug In” is sufficient only if it means the Team is currently in a valid foxhole and the relevant V4 rules permit it to be there. Unit-wide dig-in eligibility, fortification state, movement/shooting since digging in, and special rules may affect that state. For a rules-faithful resolver, request resolved `in_foxholes` / `has_bulletproof_cover_against_bombardment` or treat Dug In as the user's assertion that conditions were checked. Do not treat a bare label as a universal modifier. |
| Repeat Bombardment and persistence | `VERIFIED_V4` | A Unit with a previously Ranged In Aiming Point can fire a Repeat Bombardment at that same point on later turns, automatically Ranging In on its first attempt; the core rule states no cap on consecutive turns. Infantry successful saves are re-rolled. A Spotting Team is required for attempt accounting; FAQ confirms a Repeat uses one of the Spotter's three attempts (even though it auto-ranges in), and the Spotter can differ from the original. If Spotter cannot see the Aiming Point, add +1 To Hit. Remove the Ranged In marker if an Artillery Team moves (FAQ). Sources: rulebook “Repeat Bombardment,” p.70 (source 7), QRS pp.69–70, FAQ artillery questions. Repeated resolution carries surviving target Teams and their states into following turns. |
| Time on Target | `VERIFIED_V4` | US Artillery Units with the Time on Target special rule that Range In on the first attempt force hit Infantry or Gun Teams to re-roll successful Saves. This is specified in V4 Special Rules Book p.88, “Time on Target.” It is conditioned on both the Unit actually having the rule and first-attempt Range In; do not infer it merely from nation. |
| Repeat Bombardment and Time on Target interaction | `UNRESOLVED` | Core Repeat Bombardment automatically Ranges In on its first attempt, while ToT triggers when the Unit Ranges In on its first attempt. The core sources reviewed do not explicitly state whether a later-turn Repeat by a ToT Unit triggers Time on Target. Both effects request a successful Save re-roll, but the reviewed sources do not clarify whether multiple simultaneous re-roll effects stack. Do not assert a ToT trigger or a second re-roll on a Repeat without a source-specific ruling. |
| British — Mike Target | `VERIFIED_V4` | When a Spotting Team successfully Ranges In an Artillery Unit with Mike Target, it may immediately roll to Range In another Artillery Unit; if successful, the second Unit is treated as Ranging In on the same attempt as the first (official British V4 rules preview p.67; FAQ gives timing/example). This changes battery/ranging-in coordination, not an individual bombardment's infantry save chain. |
| Japanese — Fire Bursts and Banners | `VERIFIED_V4` | Japanese Fire Bursts: specified two-gun Japanese artillery bombardments do not re-roll hits; single-gun batteries still re-roll hits (V4 Special Rules Book p.58). Banners: the first Artillery Bombardment hit does not count toward pinning that Japanese Unit, so it takes a second hit to pin (same source, p.58). These are Unit/special-rule conditions, and the available rule text is Japanese-specific; nation alone should not activate these rules without confirming the relevant Unit has the rule. |
| Nation-specific rules outside listed cases | `UNRESOLVED` | These official sources confirm multiple special rules that alter artillery (e.g., US Time on Target, British Mike Target, Japanese Fire Bursts/Banners), but do not form an exhaustive catalog across all V4 army books, unit cards, command cards, campaigns, and updates. Nation selection by itself cannot establish which special rules the selected battery has. Minimum data for full handling: applicable V4 book/card and the firing Unit's printed special rules, plus whether each rule's stated trigger is met. Do not infer “no special rule” as an authoritative game fact; a calculator may offer an explicit ruleset selection or flag unsupported rules. |
| Supported Shooting Nation choices | `UNRESOLVED` | Official sources reviewed verify the specific artillery rules listed for US, British, and Japanese Units, but do not establish an exhaustive supported-nation list or prove that other V4 nations lack applicable Unit/card artillery rules. To select supported choices authoritatively requires a chosen V4 period/book/card corpus and a Unit-rule inventory. Until that corpus exists, expose only rules explicitly declared for the firing Unit and flag unknown coverage; do not silently label any nation's default behavior as complete. |
| Pinning and persistence | `VERIFIED_V4` | An Infantry Unit is Pinned Down by one hit from an Artillery Bombardment (QRS p.70). Pinned Down persists until Rally in its owner's Starting Step; if it remains Pinned Down, it cannot fire a Bombardment (QRS “Rally Pinned Down Units,” p.64). A later bombardment can hit/pin it again; prior pin state does not increase the one-hit threshold. Japanese Banners has the exception above. For multi-turn simulation, track the pinned state and Rally outcome between attacks. |
| Rally a Pinned Down Unit | `VERIFIED_V4` | In the Starting Step, roll one D6 for the Unit and score its Motivation rating or its separate Rally rating if different; success rallies and removes Pinned Down, failure leaves it Pinned Down (Mid-War QRS “Rally Pinned Down Units,” p.64; Early/Late QRS p.41). Use the Unit's applicable printed Rally/Motivation value including explicit modifiers; do not substitute Last Stand. Standard Command Leadership rerolls a failed Rally test when the Unit Leader is within 6in/15cm and Line of Sight of its own Formation Commander, just as for other listed Command Leadership tests (V4 rulebook, “Command Leadership”; V4 FAQ confirms command LOS ignores friendly Teams). For threshold T, base success is (7−T)/6; with eligible failed-roll reroll it is 1−(1−p)^2. No artillery-specific Rally modifier is listed in the artillery rules. |
| “Bad Spirit” / Unit In Good Spirits | `VERIFIED_V4` | “Bad Spirit” is not the core V4 term; the rule uses “not In Good Spirits.” Check Unit Last Stand in the Starting Step after remounting Bailed Out Teams. With no Teams Bailed Out or Destroyed, the Unit is In Good Spirits. Otherwise it needs 3+ Infantry Teams, 2+ non-Bailed-Out Tank Teams, or 2+ Gun Teams remaining; count only Teams In Command for these survivor criteria (FAQ clarifies In Command does not qualify the no-casualty branch). Special large-Unit rule: a Unit that started the game with at least 16 Infantry or Gun Teams needs at least 5 Infantry Teams (rather than 3) or 2 Gun Teams (EW/LW FAQ “Large infantry units”). On a mixed Unit, either applicable Infantry or Gun criterion suffices (FAQ “mixed Team types”). A Unit Leader destroyed and not replaced also makes the Unit not In Good Spirits (V4 rulebook, Unit Last Stand). A Unit not In Good Spirits rolls its Motivation/Last Stand rating; success continues and failure destroys it. |
| Unit Last Stand success probability | `VERIFIED_V4` | One D6 succeeds when result is at least the Unit's Motivation rating, or its Last Stand rating if different (QRS p.81). For threshold T from 2 through 6, success probability is (7−T)/6; e.g. Fearless 3+ = 4/6, Confident 4+ = 3/6, Reluctant 5+ = 2/6. Standard Command Leadership permits re-rolling a failed Unit Last Stand test when its Unit Leader is within 6in/15cm and Line of Sight of its own Formation Commander (V4 rulebook “Command Leadership”; FAQ confirms friendly Teams do not block this LOS). With that condition and no special exception, success chance is 1−(1−p)^2. |
| Zero remaining Teams | `VERIFIED_V4` | A Unit with no Teams left no longer exists and is Destroyed; it does not take a Last Stand test (V4 FAQ, “What if the Unit no longer has any Teams left…”). For an Infantry Unit destroyed outright by bombardment casualties, Formation state must then be recomputed before Formation Last Stand. |
| Formation destruction after casualties | `VERIFIED_V4` | Check Formation Last Stand in the Starting Step after Unit Last Stand. A Formation is In Good Spirits if at least two of its Units (HQ counts; Transport Units do not) exist on table or in Reserve; those Units need not themselves be In Good Spirits (QRS p.82; V4 FAQ). If fewer than two remain, the Formation is Destroyed and all its remaining Units are destroyed. This is general Formation Last Stand, not artillery-specific. |
| Calculate Unit/Formation destruction from a bombardment | `UNRESOLVED` | Minimum simple-case inputs: target Unit's initial size (whether it started with ≥16 Infantry/Gun Teams); current per-Team survival/casualty results and Team categories; which survivors are In Command at the next Starting Step; whether any Teams are Bailed Out; Unit Leader present/replaced state; Unit Motivation/Last Stand threshold; whether Command Leadership re-roll applies (Formation Commander distance/LOS); whether the Unit is part of a Formation; count of other extant Formation Units on table/in Reserve (HQ included, Transport Units excluded); and the Last Stand die result for an actual outcome. Also need casualty probabilities, not just a fixed “teams under template” count, if reporting probabilities. If no Teams remain, destroy the Unit immediately without Last Stand. A template count alone cannot determine destruction. |
| Between-turn Last Stand timing | `VERIFIED_V4` | Bombardment casualties happen in a Shooting Step. Unit Last Stand and Formation Last Stand checks happen in the owning player's next Starting Step, after remounting (QRS pp.39, 81–82; V4 FAQ “Last Stand”). A multi-turn bombardment sequence must process those between attacks if the turn order reaches that step; casualties do not trigger the tests immediately in the Shooting Step. |
| Multi-turn simulation horizon | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | No core-rule limit on successive repeats is stated while the same Unit retains its Ranged In marker and remains eligible to fire. For a probability engine, use a bounded user-entered number of future bombardments/turns. “Continue until no target teams remain” may fail to terminate because misses/saves can recur; game-terminal conditions depend on wider mission state. Carry surviving targets, current template coverage, pin/Rally state, marker/firing eligibility, spotting visibility, and between-turn Last Stand state. Stop at the entered horizon or earlier when a target/firing Unit/Formation is gone or repeat eligibility is lost. In a simple target-only model, target Pinned Down state does not change incoming bombardment resolution; it matters only if simulating that target Unit's own later actions (a Pinned Unit cannot Bombard). Assume the firing Artillery Unit is eligible and elects to fire each modeled turn, or supply updated eligibility per turn. |
| Single-unit destruction probability input contract | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | For a bounded model of one standard Infantry Unit under the template and no unmodeled Unit special rules, accept: (1) initial Unit size (to select the ≥16 Infantry/Gun large-Unit threshold); (2) the current/full Unit Team roster divided into under-template vs uncovered Teams (uncovered Teams remain alive and count toward Good Spirits unless already lost); (3) current destroyed/Bailed Out status and each surviving Team's In Command status at the next Starting Step; (4) whether Unit Leader is alive/replaced; (5) Unit Motivation and any distinct Last Stand rating; (6) whether standard Command Leadership applies, resolved from Unit Leader within 6in/15cm and LOS of the Formation Commander; (7) Formation membership and, if Formation destruction is requested, the post-Unit-Last-Stand existence of every other Formation Unit on table/in Reserve, including HQ and excluding Transport Units; and (8) either probability distributions for this bombardment's casualty outcomes or a realized casualty set. Apply casualties only to Teams hit under the template; then evaluate Unit In Good Spirits using all surviving Unit Teams and the criteria above. If no Team remains, destroy the Unit immediately. Otherwise, if not In Good Spirits, resolve one Last Stand D6 against the selected rating; Command Leadership re-rolls a failure when eligible. After all Unit Last Stand checks, Formation is destroyed if fewer than two Formation Units remain; all its remaining Units are destroyed. A probabilistic Formation result needs other Units' post-test existence supplied or their Last Stand states/tests modeled too. |
| Team In Command across turns | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | In Command status is determined by position at the end of a Unit's Movement Step and persists until its next Movement Step (V4 FAQ, source 11). For this calculator, require a user-resolved In Command flag at the next Last Stand check, based on that last Movement Step; remove destroyed Teams. Do not infer command from template coverage or casualty count. A static multi-turn abstraction must either hold those flags fixed (explicit assumption that no relevant movement/reorganization occurs) or request updated flags each turn. The same resolved Unit Leader/Formation Commander distance and LOS facts determine eligibility for Command Leadership rerolls on both Rally and Last Stand. |
| Infantry Save control | `VERIFIED_V4` | For an ordinary Infantry Team, Other Save is 3+ (Early/Late QRS p.39). A UI control offering 4+ or 5+ must not accept those as generic Infantry saves: those values apply to other target classes. Permit a non-3+ Infantry save only when a named and sourced V4 special rule is explicitly selected; otherwise reject/flag unsupported. Under artillery, successful save = unharmed; failed save = destroyed unless applicable Bulletproof Cover requires Firepower. |
| Current implementation | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | The current shooting core has no artillery resolution path (domain accepts normal shooting facts only). No current artillery behavior exists to compare for conflict. Existing normal shooting remains governed by the earlier sections. |

### Artillery unresolved details / implementation gates

- V1 must resolve Range In attempt number (1/2/3 or Repeat), target Team Is Hit On, firing weapon-count category (1–2 / 3–4 / 5+), and Infantry save/cover state. Infantry Other Save is normally 3+; 4+/5+ are other target classes and out of scope. The requested minimum inputs alone do not determine a V4 To Hit probability. The available authoritative source text also does not settle all-or-none artillery Unit participation; do not imply that gun count validates it.
- Repeat Bombardment is eligible on successive later turns while its Ranged In marker and firing conditions persist. It automatically ranges in on the Spotter's first attempt, uses that attempt for spotter roll accounting, rerolls infantry successful saves, and carries survivors forward. Whether repeat triggers Time on Target or whether overlapping reroll effects stack remains unresolved; do not count two sequential rerolls without an authoritative ruling.
- Special rule effects such as Brutal may also affect Infantry saves when present on the weapon profile, while ToT/Repeat Bombardment have their own triggers. The V1 ruleset must either collect the actual weapon/unit special rules or explicitly limit itself to the supported rules above; a nation selector is not itself proof of a unit rule.
- To calculate actual Unit/Formation destruction, require the post-casualty Unit composition/counts by Team category and In Command status, Team/Unit Leader states, Last Stand/Motivation and applicable special rules, Formation membership and other surviving Units, plus turn timing/die results.
- For the standard Infantry Unit threshold, input the Unit's initial Infantry/Gun Team count so the ≥16 large-Unit threshold can be selected. At the upcoming Last Stand check, use survivor count in Command; In Command status is based on the Unit's position at the end of its previous Movement Step and persists until the next Movement Step (V4 FAQ). A probability calculator can accept an explicit per-Team In Command flag at check time; it must not infer this from the template. Destroyed Teams are removed and cannot count.
- `4+` or `5+` must not be accepted as an Infantry Other Save merely because the UI offers generic save selectors: V4 Early/Late QRS assigns Infantry 3+, while 4+/5+ values refer to other target classes. Allow a different Infantry Save only when a cited, explicitly selected V4 special rule modifies it; otherwise reject/flag it as unsupported.
- For an infantry-only minimum implementation without roster geometry, a PROJECT_ASSUMPTION can take an explicit under-template count and explicit uncovered surviving-team counts by In Command status; it must not assume every Unit Team is under the template or treat uncovered members as casualties. Formation destruction probability still needs the rest of the Formation's post-Last-Stand Unit states.
- At each between-turn Starting Step in a multi-bombardment model, resolve Rally before any bombardment the Unit might fire: input/use its Rally rating (or Motivation if no separate Rally rating), Command Leadership eligibility, and the D6 result or its probability. A failed test keeps the Unit Pinned and unable to Bombard; a passed test clears pin. Do not substitute a Last Stand result or defer Rally until after that turn's shooting.
- A multi-turn simulation requires a user-entered bounded turn count; target survivors, pin state and Rally outcome; Ranged In marker/eligible battery; spotter selection and visibility at each repeat; and between-turn Last Stand resolution. Do not model “until terminal” without mission/game state.
- The QRS does not resolve all exceptional fortification/cover sources or the ToT/Repeat interaction. Classify those branches `UNRESOLVED`; do not make generic substitutions.

## Minimum verified procedure

### Shooting context, shot count, and To Hit

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shot count supplied to this calculator | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | User-entered total shots is the product contract. In the game, a Team rolls one die per point of its applicable weapon ROF; Halted ROF applies if the Team did not move and is not Pinned Down, otherwise Moving ROF applies (Mid-War QRS, “How Many Dice,” p.56; Early/Late QRS, “Rate of Fire,” p.33). The app does not infer weapon, Team, ROF, movement, or eligibility. It treats the supplied count as already resolved by the user. |
| Shooting Step sequence | `VERIFIED_V4` | The sequence includes checking range, line of sight and concealment, declaring targets, then rolling to hit, assigning hits and rolling saves (Mid-War QRS, p.49; Early/Late QRS, p.26). |
| Weapon eligibility | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | A Tank/Aircraft Team may fire all its machine-guns or one other weapon; Infantry/Gun Team may fire one weapon (Mid-War QRS, “Which Weapons Can Fire,” p.50). V1 accepts the total shot count directly and does not validate which weapons may fire or derive the shot count. |
| Baseline To Hit number | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll against the target Team's “Is Hit On” number (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). The Early/Late QRS gives Veteran 4+, Trained 3+, Conscript 2+. The user must supply the applicable base To Hit number; a generic target classification cannot be inferred from shot count. |
| Long Range modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit when range is **over** 16in/40cm (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). Exactly 16in/40cm is not “over.” Range/max weapon range eligibility itself remains user-resolved. |
| Concealed modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit if target is Concealed but not Gone to Ground; add +2 if Concealed and Gone to Ground (same To Hit headings/pages). A bare `concealed` boolean is insufficient to distinguish the +1 and +2 branches; Gone to Ground must be separately supplied or the user must supply an already determined modifier. |
| Dug In and To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | Dug In is not listed as its own To Hit modifier. Dug In may make particular Infantry, man-packed/medium Gun teams Concealed under the applicable conditions (Open Fire V4 booklet, “Digging In,” pp.14, 17; QRS concealment rule at Early/Late pp.30–31). If the target's Dug In state means it is Concealed in the actual situation, apply the Concealed/Gone to Ground rule, not a separate Dug In modifier. ### Saves, Firepower, and kills

## Current implementation audit crosswalk

The existing calculator models a generic chain of binomial hit, save-failure, and Firepower events. Ordinary single-D6 threshold arithmetic for 2+ through 6+ and the gated 7+/8+ hit probabilities are mathematically consistent with those specific dice mechanics. The following are not complete V4 outcomes: one fixed target number with no situation modifiers; one generic save threshold; unconditional Firepower after every failed save; treating every Firepower success as a kill; and interpreting whole-volley 5+/8+ hit tails as Unit pinning probabilities. Keep such legacy abstractions distinct from V4-labeled outputs until the required inputs and resolution branches above are implemented.
