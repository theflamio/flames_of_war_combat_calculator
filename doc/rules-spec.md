# Minimum V4 shooting rules specification for calculator V1

## Scope and evidence

This specification defines only the Flames of War Fourth Edition (V4) rules needed to calculate direct-fire hits, eligible pinning probabilities, and shooting kills from tabletop facts entered by the user. The product accepts a total shot count and situation facts directly; it does not identify weapons or Units or derive shots from a roster. It does not specify artillery, aircraft shooting, assaults, special rules, or every exceptional shooting case.

Each finding below has exactly one status:

- `VERIFIED_V4`: supported by the cited V4 source.
- `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION`: requested calculation convention not itself a complete game rule.
- `UNRESOLVED`: cited material does not establish required semantics or input.
- `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT`: current application behavior is incompatible with verified V4 behavior.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE`: not required for the V1 product boundary.

## Minimum verified procedure

### Shooting context, shot count, and To Hit

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shot count supplied to this calculator | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | User-entered total shots is the product contract. In the game, a Team rolls one die per point of its applicable weapon ROF; Halted ROF applies if the Team did not move and is not Pinned Down, otherwise Moving ROF applies (Mid-War QRS, “How Many Dice,” p.56; Early/Late QRS, “Rate of Fire,” p.33). The app does not infer weapon, Team, ROF, movement, or eligibility. It treats the supplied count as already resolved by the user. |
| Shooting Step sequence | `VERIFIED_V4` | The sequence includes checking range, line of sight and concealment, declaring targets, then rolling to hit, assigning hits and rolling saves (Mid-War QRS, p.49; Early/Late QRS, p.26). |
| Weapon eligibility | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | A Tank/Aircraft Team may fire all its machine-guns or one other weapon; Infantry/Gun Team may fire one weapon (Mid-War QRS, “Which Weapons Can Fire,” p.50). V1 accepts the total shot count directly and does not validate which weapons may fire or derive the shot count. |
| Baseline To Hit number | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll against the target Team's “Is Hit On” number (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). The Early/Late QRS gives Veteran 4+, Trained 3+, Conscript 2+. The user must supply the applicable base To Hit number; a generic target classification cannot be inferred from shot count. |
| Long Range modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit when range is **over** 16in/40cm (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). Exactly 16in/40cm is not “over.” Range/max weapon range eligibility itself remains user-resolved. |
| Concealed modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit if target is Concealed but not Gone to Ground; add +2 if Concealed and Gone to Ground (same To Hit headings/pages). A bare `concealed` boolean is insufficient to distinguish the +1 and +2 branches; Gone to Ground must be separately supplied or the user must supply an already determined modifier. |
| Dug In and To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | Dug In is not listed as its own To Hit modifier. Dug In may make particular Infantry, man-packed/medium Gun teams Concealed under the applicable conditions (Open Fire V4 booklet, “Digging In,” pp.14, 17; QRS concealment rule at Early/Late pp.30–31). If the target's Dug In state means it is Concealed in the actual situation, apply the Concealed/Gone to Ground rule, not a separate Dug In modifier. Do not infer that every Dug In target is Concealed: applicability depends on Team type and situation, or must be entered as the resolved Concealed fact. |
| Other core To Hit modifiers | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 for shooter moved Out of Command, shooting through Smoke, or shooting at Night (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). These are independent listed modifiers. Base number plus applicable modifiers establishes the required score. |
| 7+ / 8+ To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | If 7+ is required, roll 6 followed by 5+; if 8+ is required, roll 6 followed by 6 (Early/Late QRS, p.34; Mid-War QRS, p.56). These are gated rolls, giving probabilities 1/18 and 1/36 per shot. |
| To Hit beyond 8+, or `1+` | `UNRESOLVED` | The cited summary specifies the 7+ and 8+ cases only. It does not establish a general calculator mapping for 9+ or a selectable 1+ target. Do not silently clamp or extrapolate. Require an explicit V4 source/specification before enabling such values. |
| Adding listed modifiers and target-number cap | `UNRESOLVED` | The QRS lists the modifiers but does not, in the cited summary, settle every combination, maximum target number, or special-rule interaction. Ordinary listed modifiers may be summed for the specified basic situations; if that yields more than 8+, exact resolution semantics are not specified here. Require a supported rule clarification or mark the resulting hit calculation incomplete. |
| `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Existing selector-only To Hit behavior lacks base target state and the current-situation modifiers. Existing 7+/8+ gated-die handling matches the cited summary only when those final target numbers are already determined. |

### Saves, Firepower, and kills

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Armour Save: armored Tank Team | `VERIFIED_V4` | Target player rolls D6 and adds the target's applicable armour rating; add +1 when range is over 16in/40cm. Compare total with weapon Anti-tank: greater means no effect; equal means Firepower Test to Bail Out; less means Firepower Test to Destroy, otherwise Bail Out (Mid-War QRS, “Roll Armour Saves,” p.59; Early/Late QRS, p.36). User must supply armour face (where relevant), Anti-tank, range, and any hit-location facts required to select the armour value. |
| Other Save: class values | `VERIFIED_V4` | Early/Late QRS “Other Saves,” p.39: Infantry, Heavy Weapons and Cavalry 3+; Unarmoured Tank Teams 5+; Unarmoured Tank Teams with Gun Shield 4+; Recce Unarmoured Tank Teams 4+; Aircraft 3+. A successful save is unharmed; otherwise destroyed, except Infantry/Gun in Bulletproof Cover or Aircraft require the shooter to score Firepower to destroy. Mid-War QRS p.62 confirms the procedure but its summary does not enumerate this value table. Apply values only when the user supplies the relevant target class/state. |
| Save branch selection | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | The calculator can resolve saves only if the user declares the applicable save context and supplies the required target/weapon facts. It cannot select Armour vs Other Save from a guessed unit identity. Any abstract one-number “save” input is not itself a V4 save procedure. |
| Firepower roll | `VERIFIED_V4` | Firepower is a D6 threshold test called only in the specified branches: for equal/less Armour Save comparisons as above, and for Other Saves when Infantry/Gun is in Bulletproof Cover or the target is Aircraft (Mid-War QRS pp.59, 62; Early/Late pp.36, 39). A roll meeting the entered Firepower threshold succeeds. A failure in the equal-AT armour case leaves the tank not bailed; a failure against less-than-AT means Bail Out; failure to Firepower-destroy Infantry/Gun in Bulletproof Cover or Aircraft means the hit did not destroy it. |
| Kill result meaning | `VERIFIED_V4` | In this product, a “kill” must mean a Team destroyed by the resolved shooting hit(s), not every failed save. Armour can produce no effect, Bail Out, or destruction; Other Save failures usually destroy directly, with the Bulletproof Cover/Aircraft Firepower exception. For an armored Tank Team, `Bail Out` is a distinct non-destroyed outcome (QRS citations above). |
| Kill distribution completeness | `UNRESOLVED` | Kill probabilities/distributions cannot be calculated from only shot count, Firepower and a generic save target. They additionally require target save class, armor face/rating where applicable, Anti-tank, Bulletproof Cover/Gun Shield/Recce status where applicable, range, and a specified hit allocation/target topology. If those are absent, report the kill result as unavailable/incomplete rather than a V4 kill distribution. |
| Current generic save → Firepower → kill chain | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Running Firepower after every generic failed save and counting every Firepower success as one kill does not implement the V4 branches or distinguish bail outs, no effect, and destruction. |

### Hit allocation and pinning

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hit allocation rule | `VERIFIED_V4` | First hit must be assigned to the declared target. Remaining hits are assigned evenly among valid targets that are part of the same Unit as the target Team, the same type as that Team, and within 6in/15cm of it (Mid-War QRS, “Assign Hits,” p.58; Early/Late QRS, p.34). The QRS summary does not capture every exception. |
| Target-context and shot grouping inputs | `UNRESOLVED` | Pinning and killing need to relate possible hits to target context(s), since V4 assigns hits to eligible target Teams and pinning is counted on a Unit. V1 requires no unit name, catalog identity, or lookup: the user can provide relevant Team count/type facts and which shots are directed at the same target context. However, the cited summaries do not establish the minimum grouping/data model or how to derive hit-count distributions after even allocation across multiple valid targets. Do not invent that probability method. Until specified, any dependent pinning or kill distribution remains unresolved; clarification must define the needed user-entered grouping and how per-shot hit outcomes map to allocated hit counts. |
| Pinning threshold | `VERIFIED_V4` | A Unit is Pinned Down when it takes a total of 5 hits, or 8 hits if it has at least 12 Teams (Mid-War QRS, “Pinned Down,” p.64; Early/Late QRS, p.41). “Big platoon” is not the rule criterion; the specified fact is at least 12 Teams in the target Unit. |
| Pinning ineligibility | `VERIFIED_V4` | Armoured Tank Teams and Aircraft cannot be Pinned Down (same Pinned Down headings/pages). Therefore pinning probability is not applicable to such a target Unit. |
| Meaning of “large/big platoon” | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | If retaining legacy output labels, “big” can be presentation shorthand for user-entered target-context fact of at least 12 Teams. Do not infer this from a platoon name, formation, infantry class, or shot count. The V4 criterion refers to a Unit and its Team count, not a “big platoon”; no Unit name or identity lookup is required. |
| Pinning probability from one volley | `UNRESOLVED` | The threshold rule counts cumulative hits on a Unit. A probability from the current volley needs the relevant target-context grouping, Team-count/eligibility facts, prior hit context, and a specified relationship between per-shot hits and hits allocated under the V4 allocation rule. Total shots alone do not supply these. The source summary does not settle the minimum grouping or probability calculation; until that is specified, the requested 5/8 pinning probabilities are blocked. |
| Accumulation across multiple shooting Units | `UNRESOLVED` | The cited QRS says “total” hits on a Unit but the summary does not specify enough about timing/reset and staged shooting to define a general multi-shooter probability pool. Require an explicit current Shooting Step hit total/context from the user or a further authoritative V4 rule clarification before combining separate volleys. |
| Pinned Down follow-on effects | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | The subsequent ROF/movement restrictions do not affect this request's stated outputs for the current volley. If later used to derive shots or subsequent shooting, the Pinned Down state and Moving ROF must be handled (Mid-War QRS p.56; Early/Late p.33 and V4 FAQ). |
| Current whole-volley 5+/8+ tails | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Reporting `P(X≥5)` / `P(X≥8)` for all volley hits as pinning chances ignores Unit allocation, eligibility, 12-Team criterion, prior hits, and target context. The arithmetic tails may be retained as clearly labeled abstract statistics (`PROJECT_ASSUMPTION`), but must not be represented as V4 pinning probabilities. |

## Strictly necessary product boundary

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Unit/team identity lookup | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | Product boundary says users know the tabletop situation; the app does not identify a weapon or Unit, use a roster/catalog, or require a Unit name or identity. It asks for relevant target-context facts directly. |
| Full target eligibility, range, line of sight, terrain, and weapon-profile validation | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | These are prerequisites to a valid tabletop shot and belong to the user's resolved situation. The app's direct shot-count input does not claim to validate them. If the app later claims to validate whether a shot can be made, that needs a separate specification. |
| Terrain-derived concealment and Dug In validation | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | User may enter resolved `concealed` and `gone_to_ground` states; the calculator does not infer these from terrain geometry. A separate `dug_in` fact alone does not produce a modifier. It only matters to this requested calculation if it determines Concealed under applicable V4 conditions (Open Fire V4 booklet pp.14, 17; Early/Late QRS pp.30–31). |
| Special weapons, national rules, and exceptional target rules | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | No weapon/unit catalog is part of V1. Add only when specifically requested and supported by its own V4 rule specification. User-entered final To Hit, shot, or save facts may represent tabletop adjudication, but must not imply that exceptional cases were implemented. |
| Legacy kill-count summary cutoffs (2+, 5+) and simulation trial count | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | These are presentation/statistical choices, not general V4 thresholds. They can summarize a fully specified distribution but do not affect rule resolution. |

## Required inputs and blocked outputs

| Requested result | Status | Minimum facts beyond direct total shots | When blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Hit distribution | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | Base target Team Is Hit On; range-over-16in status; Concealed; Gone to Ground; Out of Command shooter; smoke; night; either these facts or an explicitly user-resolved final To Hit target. | Block if needed modifier/state is missing, final target exceeds specified 8+ semantics, or an unprovided special rule changes To Hit. |
| Pinning probability and 5/8 outputs | `UNRESOLVED` | Hit probability inputs; user-entered target-context grouping and relevant Team count/type/eligibility facts; prior hit context (or explicit zero); specified mapping from hit outcomes to allocated hits. No name or catalog identity is required. | Block if only total volley shots are supplied, target-context grouping or cumulative hit context is absent, or the allocation-to-probability method remains unspecified. The V4 threshold is 8 for at least 12 Teams, otherwise 5; pinning does not apply to Armoured Tank Teams or Aircraft. |
| Kill probability/distribution | `UNRESOLVED` | Hit inputs; user-entered target-context grouping; selected Armour Save or Other Save context; target class and applicable armour/Anti-tank, range, save modifiers, Bulletproof Cover/Gun Shield/Recce/Aircraft facts; Firepower; any other applicable rule facts. No Unit name or lookup is required. | Block if target grouping, target/save branch, required values, or the mapping from hits to allocated target hits is missing or unspecified. Distinguish destroyed, bailed out, unharmed/no effect outcomes; do not report one universal failed-save kill chain. |

If a requested result is blocked, mark it as unresolved/incomplete and name the missing input or unresolved rule interaction. Do not substitute a default target class, unit size, target allocation, prior-hit count, modifier, or save branch.

## Current implementation audit crosswalk

The existing calculator models a generic chain of binomial hit, save-failure, and Firepower events. Ordinary single-D6 threshold arithmetic for 2+ through 6+ and the gated 7+/8+ hit probabilities are mathematically consistent with those specific dice mechanics. The following are not complete V4 outcomes: one fixed target number with no situation modifiers; one generic save threshold; unconditional Firepower after every failed save; treating every Firepower success as a kill; and interpreting whole-volley 5+/8+ hit tails as Unit pinning probabilities. Keep such legacy abstractions distinct from V4-labeled outputs until the required inputs and resolution branches above are implemented.
