# Minimum V4 shooting rules specification for calculator V1

## Scope and evidence

This specification defines Flames of War Fourth Edition (V4) shooting rules relevant to the current direct-fire calculator and the requested infantry artillery calculator. The direct-fire product accepts resolved facts from the user; it does not identify weapons or Units. Artillery facts below cover only bombardments against Infantry Teams. It does not specify aircraft shooting, assaults, or vehicle-target resolution.

## Artillery bombardment against Infantry Teams (V1)

The official QRS is a summary, not the complete rulebook. The following is limited to what its V4 artillery headings establish. Do not infer a full game simulation from the bombardment probability calculator; targeting legality, spotting, and many army-book special rules require more inputs than the requested V1 interface.

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible bombardment | `VERIFIED_V4` | Any Team with an Artillery weapon (ROF marked “artillery” or “salvo”) may fire a Bombardment instead of Shooting; a Unit with Artillery weapons is an Artillery Unit (V4 rulebook p.66, “Who Can Bombard,” official extract source 7). This means “number of guns firing” is an input to the dice modifier but does not by itself establish weapon eligibility or Unit membership. Whether all artillery weapons in a Unit must participate together is not established by the available official summary/extract and remains unresolved for this spec. |
| Aiming point and template | `VERIFIED_V4` | The Spotting Team selects an Aiming Point in its Line of Sight; an Artillery Template is 6in/15cm square and a Salvo Template is 10in/25cm square. Template danger-close restrictions: 4in/10cm for Artillery, 6in/15cm for Salvo, 8in/20cm for aircraft (V4 rulebook pp.66, 100, official extract source 7; QRS “Select Aiming Point,” p.67). V1 accepts the already-established number of Infantry Teams under the template; it does not calculate table geometry or validate the aiming point. |
| Range In and attempts | `VERIFIED_V4` | Make up to three Range In attempts, rolling against the Artillery Unit's Skill; +1 to the score required if the template would cover Short or Tall Terrain or Smoke, and +1 at Night (QRS p.68). Success on attempt one has no hit penalty; attempt two adds +1 to score needed To Hit; attempt three adds +2 (QRS p.69). |
| Teams under template | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll separately to hit each Team under the template (QRS “Roll to Hit,” p.69; Early/Late QRS p.48: “all Teams under Template”). Whether a base only partially covered by the template counts as under it is not explicit in the cited official summaries and remains `UNRESOLVED`. For probability arithmetic, supplied team count means teams adjudicated by the player as under the template. |
| Artillery To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | Base is the target Team’s Is Hit On number (Veteran 4+, Trained 3+, Conscript 2+); add +1 for ranging in on attempt two or a Repeat Bombardment whose Spotting Team cannot see the Aiming Point; add +2 for attempt three (QRS p.69). The Artillery To Hit target is not the Direct Fire To Hit target and does not use its normal long-range/concealment modifiers. |
| Number of weapons firing | `VERIFIED_V4` | With 1 or 2 weapons, re-roll successful To Hit rolls; with 5 or more, re-roll failed To Hit rolls (QRS p.69, “Number of Weapons Firing”). The sheet states no modifier for 3–4 weapons. Re-rolls affect each eligible hit die; they are not extra shots. |
| Infantry Firepower / weapon ratings | `VERIFIED_V4` | Bombardment profiles have artillery Anti-tank and Firepower ratings, and the QRS provides adjusted bombardment rating tables (Early/Late QRS p.48). Infantry V1 does not need Anti-tank. The adjusted artillery Firepower table is: profile FP 1+→1+, 2+→2+, 3+→3+, 4+→3+, 5+→4+, 6→4+ (Early/Late QRS p.48, “Arsenal Firepower / Adjusted Firepower”). Use the adjusted value as the Firepower test threshold when that test is called for. |
| Infantry saves, casualty and Firepower | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll saves as for Shooting, with artillery-specific exceptions: the artillery QRS says there is no Bulletproof Cover behind terrain (QRS p.70; rulebook “Roll Saves,” p.70, source 7). Standard Infantry Other Save is 3+ (Early/Late QRS p.39). Under the standard Other Save rule, Infantry in Bulletproof Cover require a successful weapon Firepower test after failing their save to be destroyed (Early/Late QRS p.39; V4 FAQ “Bulletproof Cover”). Artillery's terrain exception means do not grant that cover solely because of terrain. The QRS does not establish how every non-terrain defensive rule that grants a save/cover interacts with bombardment; see unresolved row. The artillery Firepower rating table is not a universal extra roll against ordinary exposed Infantry. |
| Repeat Bombardment | `VERIFIED_V4` | A previously Ranged In bombardment may be fired again on the same Aiming Point in a later turn; it is treated as having Ranged In on the first attempt, but the +1 To Hit penalty applies if its Spotting Team cannot see the Aiming Point. Infantry (and Gun) successful saves are re-rolled. Sources: QRS p.69–70; V4 *Lessons From The Front* FAQ, artillery/repeat bombardment questions. A spotting Team is still required; FAQ confirms it can differ from the original Spotting Team, and repeats consume Range In attempts for further batteries. |
| Time on Target | `VERIFIED_V4` | US Artillery Units with the Time on Target special rule that Range In on the first attempt force hit Infantry or Gun Teams to re-roll successful Saves. This is specified in V4 Special Rules Book p.88, “Time on Target.” It is conditioned on both the Unit actually having the rule and first-attempt Range In; do not infer it merely from nation. Apply as a save re-roll effect in the artillery resolution. |
| United States — Time on Target | `VERIFIED_V4` | An Artillery Unit with the rule that Ranges In on its first attempt makes hit Infantry/Gun Teams re-roll successful Saves (V4 Special Rules Book p.88). This belongs to a Unit that has the special rule, not every US bombardment solely by nation selection. |
| British — Mike Target | `VERIFIED_V4` | When a Spotting Team successfully Ranges In an Artillery Unit with Mike Target, it may immediately roll to Range In another Artillery Unit; if successful, the second Unit is treated as Ranging In on the same attempt as the first (official British V4 rules preview p.67; FAQ gives timing/example). This changes battery/ranging-in coordination, not an individual bombardment's infantry save chain. |
| Japanese — Fire Bursts and Banners | `VERIFIED_V4` | Japanese Fire Bursts: specified two-gun Japanese artillery bombardments do not re-roll hits; single-gun batteries still re-roll hits (V4 Special Rules Book p.58). Banners: the first Artillery Bombardment hit does not count toward pinning that Japanese Unit, so it takes a second hit to pin (same source, p.58). These are Unit/special-rule conditions, and the available rule text is Japanese-specific; nation alone should not activate these rules without confirming the relevant Unit has the rule. |
| Nation-specific rules outside listed cases | `UNRESOLVED` | These official sources confirm multiple special rules that alter artillery (e.g., US Time on Target, British Mike Target, Japanese Fire Bursts/Banners), but do not form an exhaustive catalog across all V4 army books, unit cards, command cards, campaigns, and updates. Nation selection by itself cannot establish which special rules the selected battery has. Minimum data for full handling: applicable V4 book/card and the firing Unit's printed special rules, plus whether each rule's stated trigger is met. Do not infer “no special rule” as an authoritative game fact; a calculator may offer an explicit ruleset selection or flag unsupported rules. |
| Pinning | `VERIFIED_V4` | An Infantry Unit is Pinned Down by one hit from an Artillery Bombardment (QRS p.70, “Pinned Down”). One or more hits to that Unit therefore satisfy the artillery pinning threshold; this is distinct from the normal shooting 5/8 hit threshold. QRS is explicit about one hit, but pinning is a Unit state, not a per-Team casualty. |
| “Bad Spirit” / Unit destruction | `UNRESOLVED` | “Bad Spirit” is not the term used by the cited core V4 QRS or FAQ; the V4 core term is “not In Good Spirits.” A Unit not In Good Spirits takes a Unit Last Stand test in the Starting Step and is Destroyed if it fails; the core rule determines Good Spirits from the remaining Team types/count (QRS p.81, source 9). This is a general morale/turn-start rule, not an artillery-specific effect. |
| Formation destruction after casualties | `VERIFIED_V4` | A Formation is In Good Spirits if it has at least two Units (including HQ, excluding Transport Units) on table or in Reserve; if not, the Formation is Destroyed (QRS p.82; V4 FAQ confirms the two Units need not themselves be In Good Spirits). This is general Formation Last Stand, not an artillery rule and not immediately rolled per bombardment. |
| Calculate destruction from a bombardment | `UNRESOLVED` | The artillery output's team casualties can be calculated only with per-Team hit/save context. Whether casualties make the target Unit not In Good Spirits requires the Unit's post-casualty remaining team counts by category (and relevant In Command/Unit Leader conditions), its Last Stand/Motivation rating and any applicable special rule; whether its Formation is destroyed additionally requires the count of other Formation Units on table/in Reserve and Transport exclusions. Actual Unit destruction then requires turn timing and the Last Stand roll unless Formation Last Stand destroys it. A template count alone is insufficient. |
| Simulation stopping condition | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | For a V1 probability calculation of one bombardment, stop after every Infantry Team under the supplied template has had its hit and save/casualty resolution. Repeat Bombardment is a separate later-turn attack, not an automatic continuation inside the same volley; expose it only as a user-selected scenario input. This is a calculation boundary, not an additional game rule. |
| Current implementation | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | The current shooting core has no artillery resolution path (domain accepts normal shooting facts only). No current artillery behavior exists to compare for conflict. Existing normal shooting remains governed by the earlier sections. |

### Artillery unresolved details / implementation gates

- V1 must obtain or otherwise explicitly resolve Range In attempt number (1/2/3 or Repeat Bombardment), the target Team's Is Hit On number, and the firing weapon count category (1–2 / 3–4 / 5+); the requested minimum inputs do not alone determine a V4 To Hit probability.
- Repeat Bombardment and Time on Target can both appear relevant to successful saves, but the official V4 rulebook/FAQ material reviewed does not expressly settle whether overlapping instructions produce one or multiple re-rolls. The FAQ confirms a bombardment already marked Ranged In from the previous turn is a Repeat Bombardment even on the first turn it is fired (FAQ, Artillery question). Until an exact ruling resolves simultaneous triggers, model both as “re-roll successful save” effects and do not apply sequential re-rolls; mark the overlap unresolved for authoritative output.
- Special rule effects such as Brutal may also affect Infantry saves when present on the weapon profile, while ToT/Repeat Bombardment have their own triggers. The V1 ruleset must either collect the actual weapon/unit special rules or explicitly limit itself to the supported rules above; a nation selector is not itself proof of a unit rule.
- To calculate kills/platoon destruction rather than individual hit/survival probabilities, V1 needs target save context and Unit state. The requested input of “number of Infantry Teams under template” does not establish that all members of one platoon are under it or how hits/casualties affect Last Stand.
- The QRS excerpts establish infantry hit/saves and pinning but not all special cases such as Infantry in buildings/foxholes under every bombardment and weapon-specific Firepower exceptions. Until an authoritative V4 citation resolves a case, classify that case `UNRESOLVED`; do not make a generic normal-shooting substitution.

## Minimum verified procedure

### Shooting context, shot count, and To Hit

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shot count supplied to this calculator | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | User-entered total shots is the product contract. In the game, a Team rolls one die per point of its applicable weapon ROF; Halted ROF applies if the Team did not move and is not Pinned Down, otherwise Moving ROF applies (Mid-War QRS, “How Many Dice,” p.56; Early/Late QRS, “Rate of Fire,” p.33). The app does not infer weapon, Team, ROF, movement, or eligibility. It treats the supplied count as already resolved by the user. |
| Shooting Step sequence | `VERIFIED_V4` | The sequence includes checking range, line of sight and concealment, declaring targets, then rolling to hit, assigning hits and rolling saves (Mid-War QRS, p.49; Early/Late QRS, p.26). |
| Weapon eligibility | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | A Tank/Aircraft Team may fire all its machine-guns or one other weapon; Infantry/Gun Team may fire one weapon (Mid-War QRS, “Which Weapons Can Fire,” p.50). V1 accepts the total shot count directly and does not validate which weapons may fire or derive the shot count. |
| Baseline To Hit number | `VERIFIED_V4` | Roll against the target Team's “Is Hit On” number (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). The Early/Late QRS gives Veteran 4+, Trained 3+, Conscript 2+. The user must supply the applicable base To Hit number; a generic target classification cannot be inferred from shot count. |
| Long Range modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit when range is **over** 16in/40cm (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). Exactly 16in/40cm is not “over.” Range/max weapon range eligibility itself remains user-resolved. |
| Concealed modifier | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 to the score needed to hit if target is Concealed but not Gone to Ground; add +2 if Concealed and Gone to Ground (same To Hit headings/pages). A bare `concealed` boolean is insufficient to distinguish the +1 and +2 branches; Gone to Ground must be separately supplied or the user must supply an already determined modifier. |
| Dug In and To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | Dug In is not listed as its own To Hit modifier. Dug In may make particular Infantry, man-packed/medium Gun teams Concealed under the applicable conditions (Open Fire V4 booklet, “Digging In,” pp.14, 17; QRS concealment rule at Early/Late pp.30–31). If the target's Dug In state means it is Concealed in the actual situation, apply the Concealed/Gone to Ground rule, not a separate Dug In modifier. Do not infer that every Dug In target is Concealed: applicability depends on Team type and situation, or must be entered as the resolved Concealed fact. |
| Other core To Hit modifiers | `VERIFIED_V4` | Add +1 for shooter moved Out of Command, shooting through Smoke, or shooting at Night (Early/Late QRS, “Score To Hit,” p.33; Mid-War QRS, p.56). These are independent listed modifiers. Base number plus applicable modifiers establishes the required score. |
| 7+ / 8+ To Hit | `VERIFIED_V4` | If 7+ is required, roll 6 followed by 5+; if 8+ is required, roll 6 followed by 6 (Early/Late QRS, p.34; Mid-War QRS, p.56). These are gated rolls, giving probabilities 1/18 and 1/36 per shot. |
| To Hit beyond 8+, or `1+` | `UNRESOLVED` | The cited summary specifies the 7+ and 8+ cases only. It does not establish a general calculator mapping for 9+ or a selectable 1+ target. Do not silently clamp or extrapolate. Require an explicit V4 source/specification before enabling such values. |
| Adding listed modifiers and target-number cap | `UNRESOLVED` | The QRS lists the modifiers but does not, in the cited summary, settle every combination, maximum target number, or special-rule interaction. Ordinary listed modifiers may be summed for the specified basic situations; if that yields more than 8+, exact resolution semantics are not specified here. Require a supported rule clarification or mark the resulting hit calculation incomplete. |
| `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Existing selector-only To Hit behavior lacks base target state and the current-situation modifiers. Existing 7+/8+ gated-die handling matches the cited summary only when those final target numbers are already determined. |

### Saves, Firepower, and kills

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Armour Save: armored Tank Team | `VERIFIED_V4` | Target player rolls D6 and adds the target's applicable armour rating; add +1 when range is over 16in/40cm. Compare total with weapon Anti-tank: greater means no effect; equal means Firepower Test to Bail Out; less means Firepower Test to Destroy, otherwise Bail Out (Mid-War QRS, “Roll Armour Saves,” p.59; Early/Late QRS, p.36). User must supply armour face (where relevant), Anti-tank, range, and any hit-location facts required to select the armour value. |
| Other Save: class values | `VERIFIED_V4` | Early/Late QRS “Other Saves,” p.39: Infantry, Heavy Weapons and Cavalry 3+; Unarmoured Tank Teams 5+; Unarmoured Tank Teams with Gun Shield 4+; Recce Unarmoured Tank Teams 4+; Aircraft 3+. A successful save is unharmed; otherwise destroyed, except Infantry/Gun in Bulletproof Cover or Aircraft require the shooter to score Firepower to destroy. Mid-War QRS p.62 confirms the procedure but its summary does not enumerate this value table. Apply values only when the user supplies the relevant target class/state. |
| Save branch selection | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | The calculator can resolve saves only if the user declares the applicable save context and supplies the required target/weapon facts. It cannot select Armour vs Other Save from a guessed unit identity. Any abstract one-number “save” input is not itself a V4 save procedure. |
| Firepower roll | `VERIFIED_V4` | Firepower is a D6 threshold test called only in the specified branches: for equal/less Armour Save comparisons as above, and for Other Saves when Infantry/Gun is in Bulletproof Cover or the target is Aircraft (Mid-War QRS pp.59, 62; Early/Late pp.36, 39). A roll meeting the entered Firepower threshold succeeds. A failure in the equal-AT armour case leaves the tank not bailed; a failure against less-than-AT means Bail Out; failure to Firepower-destroy Infantry/Gun in Bulletproof Cover or Aircraft means the hit did not destroy it. |
| Kill result meaning | `VERIFIED_V4` | In this product, a “kill” must mean a Team destroyed by the resolved shooting hit(s), not every failed save. Armour can produce no effect, Bail Out, or destruction; Other Save failures usually destroy directly, with the Bulletproof Cover/Aircraft Firepower exception. For an armored Tank Team, `Bail Out` is a distinct non-destroyed outcome (QRS citations above). |
| Kill distribution completeness | `UNRESOLVED` | Kill probabilities/distributions cannot be calculated from only shot count, Firepower and a generic save target. They additionally require target save class, armor face/rating where applicable, Anti-tank, Bulletproof Cover/Gun Shield/Recce status where applicable, range, and a specified hit allocation/target topology. If those are absent, report the kill result as unavailable/incomplete rather than a V4 kill distribution. |
| Current generic save → Firepower → kill chain | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Running Firepower after every generic failed save and counting every Firepower success as one kill does not implement the V4 branches or distinguish bail outs, no effect, and destruction. |

### Hit allocation and pinning

| Finding | Status | Rule / product treatment and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hit allocation rule | `VERIFIED_V4` | First hit must be assigned to the declared target. Remaining hits are assigned evenly among valid targets that are part of the same Unit as the target Team, the same type as that Team, and within 6in/15cm of it (Mid-War QRS, “Assign Hits,” p.58; Early/Late QRS, p.34). The QRS summary does not capture every exception. |
| Target-context and shot grouping inputs | `UNRESOLVED` | Pinning and killing need to relate possible hits to target context(s), since V4 assigns hits to eligible target Teams and pinning is counted on a Unit. V1 requires no unit name, catalog identity, or lookup: the user can provide relevant Team count/type facts and which shots are directed at the same target context. However, the cited summaries do not establish the minimum grouping/data model or how to derive hit-count distributions after even allocation across multiple valid targets. Do not invent that probability method. Until specified, any dependent pinning or kill distribution remains unresolved; clarification must define the needed user-entered grouping and how per-shot hit outcomes map to allocated hit counts. |
| Pinning threshold | `VERIFIED_V4` | A Unit is Pinned Down when it takes a total of 5 hits, or 8 hits if it has at least 12 Teams (Mid-War QRS, “Pinned Down,” p.64; Early/Late QRS, p.41). “Big platoon” is not the rule criterion; the specified fact is at least 12 Teams in the target Unit. |
| Pinning ineligibility | `VERIFIED_V4` | Armoured Tank Teams and Aircraft cannot be Pinned Down (same Pinned Down headings/pages). Therefore pinning probability is not applicable to such a target Unit. |
| Meaning of “large/big platoon” | `PROJECT_ASSUMPTION` | If retaining legacy output labels, “big” can be presentation shorthand for user-entered target-context fact of at least 12 Teams. Do not infer this from a platoon name, formation, infantry class, or shot count. The V4 criterion refers to a Unit and its Team count, not a “big platoon”; no Unit name or identity lookup is required. |
| Pinning probability from one volley | `UNRESOLVED` | The threshold rule counts cumulative hits on a Unit. A probability from the current volley needs the relevant target-context grouping, Team-count/eligibility facts, prior hit context, and a specified relationship between per-shot hits and hits allocated under the V4 allocation rule. Total shots alone do not supply these. The source summary does not settle the minimum grouping or probability calculation; until that is specified, the requested 5/8 pinning probabilities are blocked. |
| Accumulation across multiple shooting Units | `UNRESOLVED` | The cited QRS says “total” hits on a Unit but the summary does not specify enough about timing/reset and staged shooting to define a general multi-shooter probability pool. Require an explicit current Shooting Step hit total/context from the user or a further authoritative V4 rule clarification before combining separate volleys. |
| Pinned Down follow-on effects | `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | The subsequent ROF/movement restrictions do not affect this request's stated outputs for the current volley. If later used to derive shots or subsequent shooting, the Pinned Down state and Moving ROF must be handled (Mid-War QRS p.56; Early/Late p.33 and V4 FAQ). |
| Current whole-volley 5+/8+ tails | `IMPLEMENTATION_CONFLICT` | Reporting `P(X≥5)` / `P(X≥8)` for all volley hits as pinning chances ignores Unit allocation, eligibility, 12-Team criterion, prior hits, and target context. The arithmetic tails may be retained as clearly labeled abstract statistics (`PROJECT_ASSUMPTION`), but must not be represented as V4 pinning probabilities. |
